So when this project says "monitor"

Everything this project has genuinely traced — token ids, ggml op names, CUDA kernel names, sampling probabilities — sits on one side of a real line: things with a key. We know exactly what MUL_MAT means, exactly what token 43 means, exactly what a 3.6% sampling probability means, because a legend exists for each of them, written by a person or fixed by the math itself.

The {n_dims} numbers above sit on the other side. They are just as real, just as capturable, just as honestly reported — and they mean nothing we can currently read, because the index that would let us read them was never written and doesn't fall out of training as a byproduct. Calling that gap "reasoning" and calling our access to it "monitoring" borrows the vocabulary of a process we can actually watch, and lends it to one we can only watch the outside of.
